**AI-Augmented Software Engineering — Fall 2026**

| | |

| **When** | Thursday, November 12, 2026, 16:00–18:00 |
| **Format** | 25–30 multiple-choice questions, 2 hours, closed book |
| **Notes** | You may bring handwritten notes. Bring **two identical handwritten copies**: you keep one during the exam and we collect the other. |
| **Weight** | 30% of the final grade |
| **Covers** | Lectures 1–6 and **one required paper**: [*Lost in the Middle*](required-paper.md) (Liu et al., 2023) |
| **AI use** | None. The midterm is your own work. |

The exam tests whether you understood the *ideas*, not whether you memorized slide text. Most questions ask you to pick the right concept for a situation, tell two similar terms apart, or recall a specific claim from the required paper. Expect roughly five questions per week, with a few that cut across weeks.

---

## How to use this guide

1. Re-read each lecture's slides once (links below), then work through that week's section here.
2. For each **Key terms** entry, make sure you can define it in one sentence *and* say how it differs from its neighbours.
3. Read the [required paper](required-paper.md) using the three-pass method on that page. The exam asks about its main claim, its evidence and what it means for working with agents, not its fine details. The other readings in each week's section are background: useful, but not examined on their own.
4. Finish with the [self-check questions](self-check-questions.md). If you cannot answer one, go back to that week.

The six lectures were taught in this order: Week 1, Week 2, Week 3, Context Management, Codebase Structure, Code Review and Testing. The repository folders number Context Management and Codebase Structure the other way round (`05` and `04`). Nothing on the exam depends on the week numbers.
